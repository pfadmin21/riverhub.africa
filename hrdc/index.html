How to claim

Four steps.
Simple process.

The refund is claimed by the employer on behalf of the participant. We provide all the documentation your HR team needs.

1
Company submits G1 form
Minimum 5 working days before the course start date. We provide all course details needed for the form on request. Email us and we send it within 24 hours.
2
Attend the session
On-site at River Hub or online. Attendance is recorded for both modes — both qualify equally for the refund. We issue an attendance certificate on the day.
3
Company submits G3 form
Post-course, with invoice, receipt, and attendance record. We provide the platform attendance log for online participants automatically within 48 hours of the session.
4
Refund processed
Typically 4–8 weeks. Refund goes directly to the employer. Minimum 3 months of levy contribution required before the first claim can be submitted.

Eligibility

Who can
claim?

Banks & financial institutions
Any bank, insurer, or financial institution registered in Mauritius and contributing to the HRDC levy. Covers commercial banks, investment banks, insurance companies, and leasing companies.
Fund managers & administrators
Fund management companies, CIS operators, fund administrators, and Global Business companies domiciled in Mauritius. Staff attending any River Hub session qualify.
Law firms & advisory firms
Law firms, accounting firms, management consultants, and advisory businesses with Mauritius-registered employees contributing to the HRDC levy through payroll.
Regulators & government bodies
FSC, Bank of Mauritius, BOI, and other public sector entities may qualify depending on their levy contribution status. Contact HRDC directly to confirm eligibility before submitting.
Online participants from Mauritius
Mauritius-based participants attending River Hub online are equally eligible for the HRDC refund. The platform attendance log we issue within 48 hours serves as the attendance record for the G3 form.
Not sure if you qualify?
Email us with your company name and sector. We check eligibility on your behalf and advise within 24 hours. Most Mauritius-registered companies with payroll employees contribute to the levy automatically.
Common questions

HRDC
FAQ

Does the online fee qualify for HRDC, or only the on-site fee? +
Both qualify equally. The HRDC levy scheme does not distinguish between in-room and online delivery — what matters is that the training is MQA-approved and the employer submits the correct forms. River Hub's CLBS partnership covers both delivery modes.
Can we claim for multiple sessions in the same year? +
Yes. There is no cap on the number of sessions per employee per year under the standard levy scheme, subject to your employer's total levy contribution. Each session requires its own G1 and G3 submission. We batch-issue the documentation if you register for multiple sessions at once.
What if my company has only been registered for a few months? +
A minimum of 3 months of levy contribution is required before the first claim can be submitted. If your company is newer than 3 months, you can still attend and pay the full fee — and submit the claim once the 3-month threshold is reached.
Who submits the forms — me or my employer? +
Your employer (HR or finance team) submits both the G1 (before the course) and the G3 (after the course). You attend and collect your certificate. We provide your employer with a pre-filled course information pack to make the G1 submission straightforward.
How long does it take to receive the refund? +
HRDC typically processes refunds within 4–8 weeks of receiving a complete G3 submission. Processing times can vary during peak periods. The refund is paid directly to the employer, not the participant.
Can international participants claim HRDC? +
No. The HRDC levy scheme is available to Mauritius-registered employers only. International participants from Africa, Europe, the Gulf, and Asia pay the online fee with no levy refund. The regional online fee is priced accordingly to reflect this.
