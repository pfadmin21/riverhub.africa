What makes it different
A destination venue — not a generic meeting room

The same room used by the TAP Fund's Technical Assistance Facility and the African Circuit. River Hub participants travel to Tamarin for sessions — your team will too.

The west coast setting — away from Port Louis and the business district — creates a genuine change of environment. Strategy days and leadership offsites produce better results when the space signals that something different is happening.

Availability. River Hub is available for corporate hire on days outside the programme calendar. Bookings are confirmed on a first-come basis. A minimum of 5 working days' notice is required. No external catering is permitted on site.
